Customer Success Operations Analyst

We are looking for a bilingual Customer Success Operations Analyst to support customer support workflows, process documentation, CRM data quality, Excel reporting, and cross-functional communication.

Responsibilities:
- Review customer support cases and identify repeated workflow issues.
- Build clear process documentation and checklists for operations teams.
- Use Excel and basic SQL to prepare reports and improve data accuracy.
- Communicate clearly with internal stakeholders in English and Spanish.
- Support troubleshooting, escalation notes, and customer follow-up.

Preferred skills:
- Customer success or customer support experience.
- Excel, SQL basics, CRM tools, and reporting.
- Strong written communication and attention to detail.
